This policy applies to the Conversation Transcriber app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. The app offers a separate interface language setting and speech recognition language setting.

Audio and conversation processing

Speech recognition and speaker diarization run on the device you are using. The app does not send audio, transcripts, or speaker labels to the developer's server or an external speech recognition service. No account is required.

The microphone is used when you start recording. For file analysis, the app reads the audio or video file you choose. The app does not use Apple's speech recognition service.

Model downloads

The app downloads required model files from Hugging Face only when you explicitly start a download in the model management screen. Standard connection information, such as your IP address, may be visible to that service during the download. Audio and transcripts are not included in model download requests. Once the models have been downloaded, the main inference features do not require a network connection.

On-device storage and deletion

Transcripts, speaker information, and your edits are saved in the app's local data area on your device. Microphone recordings are not saved as audio files. Copies of imported audio or video are not saved by default; they are saved only if you enable that setting. If audio is not saved, playback and reanalysis after the app closes are unavailable.

Deleting a conversation in the app deletes its transcript and any audio copy you chose to save for that conversation. You can delete downloaded models in the model management screen. Normally, deleting the app also deletes data in its dedicated local storage. Conversation data and saved audio copies may be included in device backups depending on your device and backup settings.

You choose where exported files are saved and with whom they are shared. The app does not implement its own automatic iCloud or CloudKit sync.

Analytics and external services

The initial version has no advertising, usage analytics SDK, or external crash reporting SDK. The developer does not collect conversation content from the app.

Changes and contact

If these practices change, this page and its update date will be revised. See the support page for contact details.
