Contact

For bugs, feature feedback, or privacy questions, email kondofumikazu@icloud.com. You can also open a GitHub Issue; a GitHub account is required.

Issues are public. Do not post audio, transcripts, names, contact details, or other personal information there. For a private inquiry, use email.

Common tasks

  • Download both required models from the model management screen before first use.
  • After downloading the models, transcription and speaker diarization run on your device without a network connection.
  • Microphone audio is not saved. Imported audio copies remain only if you enable that setting.

Helpful details when contacting us

Include your device, OS version, app version, and any error message. You do not need to send the conversation or audio itself. You can select the app interface language separately from the speech recognition language.
